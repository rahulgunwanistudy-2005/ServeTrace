# ServeTrace — Project Bible (save as `CLAUDE.md` in repo root)

## 1. Mission
ServeTrace helps a person who lost a court case by default, because they were never actually served, prove it. They upload the process server's **affidavit of service** and their own **location history**. ServeTrace shows whether their data conflicts with the sworn claim, checks the affidavit against the service rules, and produces an evidence packet plus a draft supporting affidavit for a motion to vacate.

A second mode, **Advocate mode**, lets a legal-aid worker load many service records from one process server and finds physically impossible sequences (two services miles apart minutes apart).

Tagline: **"They swore they served you. Your phone says otherwise."**

|---|---|---|
| Impact & feasibility (25%) | >70% of debt suits end in default (Pew). NYC: ~17% of 366k consumer credit suits answered 2019–23, ~152k default judgments 2019–24 (New York Focus). Relief path already exists in law (CPLR 5015(a)(4), 317). | Target one real jurisdiction deeply: **New York City consumer credit cases**. |
| Technical execution (25%) | Structured extraction + space-time feasibility engine + batch impossible-travel graph, with a published eval. | Deterministic engine, typed contracts, tests, eval report. It must *work* live. |
| UX for non-lawyers (20%) | Three steps, one verdict, one download. | Plain language everywhere, no legal jargon without a one-line explanation, mobile-first. |
| Innovation (15%) | "Impossible travel" is the velocity check fraud engineers run on cards. Nobody applies it to affidavits of service. | Say this explicitly in the video and write-up. |
| Presentation & docs (15%) | One unforgettable map moment. Transparent disclosure. | Demo mode that never fails. Disclosure section in Devpost. |

Judges are mostly engineers and PMs from big tech. They reward: a real user, a working product, clean engineering, a clever technical angle, honest numbers.

## 3. Scope
### In scope (v1)
- Jurisdiction: **New York City Civil Court, consumer credit actions**, service on natural persons under CPLR 308(1), 308(2), 308(4).
- Defendant mode: affidavit upload → extraction → user confirms fields → location ingest (browser) → household roster (optional) → analysis → evidence packet PDF + draft supporting affidavit + deadline clock + where to get help.
- Advocate mode: CSV/XLSX of service records (or multiple affidavits) → per-server impossible-travel report + map.
- Methodology page (thresholds, eval numbers, limitations) and Privacy page.
- Demo mode with bundled synthetic cases, zero network dependency.

### Out of scope (do not build)
- Accounts, auth, database, payments, e-filing, emailing anyone, submitting complaints on the user's behalf.
- Other states. (Architecture keeps rules in one module so they can be added later.)
- Any "you will win" prediction or outcome scoring.
- Face analysis, photo analysis of people, skin-color inference. Never.
- Free-text legal advice generated by an LLM.

## 5. Legal model (the ONLY legal facts the product may state)
All legal statements in UI copy and generated documents must trace to this section. Everything generated is labelled **DRAFT — not legal advice. Review with the NYC Civil Court Help Center or a legal aid organization.**

| ID | Fact encoded | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | CPLR 308(1): personal delivery to the defendant. | method classification, description check against defendant |
| L2 | CPLR 308(2): delivery to a person of suitable age and discretion at the defendant's dwelling/abode/business **and** mailing; delivery and mailing within **20 days** of each other; proof of service filed within **20 days** of the later; service complete **10 days** after filing. | timing rules R-T1..R-T3 |
| L3 | CPLR 308(4) ("affix and mail"): allowed only when 308(1) and 308(2) cannot be made with **due diligence**; affixing and mailing within 20 days of each other; proof filed within 20 days. | timing rules, due-diligence pattern R-D1 |
| L4 | Due diligence in practice: courts expect documented attempts at varying times and days; a common practitioner standard is **three attempts on at least two different days at different times of day**. Present as "courts commonly expect", never as a statute. | R-D1 (flag for review only) |
| L5 | CPLR 5015(a)(4): a judgment may be vacated for lack of jurisdiction (e.g., improper service). No one-year limit stated for this ground; act promptly. A disputed service claim may lead the court to order a **traverse hearing**. | draft affidavit grounds, next-steps copy |
| L6 | CPLR 317: a person served other than by personal delivery who did not personally receive notice in time to defend may move within **1 year after learning of the judgment**, and no more than **5 years after entry**, with a meritorious defense. | deadline clock, alternative ground |
| L7 | NYC licensed process servers must carry a device that electronically records GPS location, date and time of each service or attempt (NYC Admin Code § 20-410; 6 RCNY § 2-233b). DCWP accepts complaints from legal advocates. | advocate-mode copy, "request the server's GPS record" next step |

## 6. Result semantics (copy discipline)
ServeTrace never says anyone lied or committed fraud. It says **data conflicts**.

| Tier | Meaning | Plain-language headline |
|---|---|---|
| `CONTRADICTED` | User's location data places them far from the claimed service point at the claimed time, beyond conservative thresholds. | "Your location data conflicts with the affidavit." |
| `CONSISTENT` | User's data places them at or near the claimed point at the claimed time. | "Your location data is consistent with the affidavit." (Show this honestly. It is a feature.) |
| `NO_DATA` | No usable location fixes near the claimed time. | "We don't have location data for that time." |
| `INCONCLUSIVE` | Data exists but neither conflicts nor matches under thresholds. | "Your data doesn't settle this either way." |

Every result card ends with the limitation line: *"Location history shows where your phone was, not proof of where you were. A judge decides what happened."*

## 7. Architecture
```
Browser (SvelteKit, static)                         Server (FastAPI, stateless)
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐            ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Wizard UI + MapLibre                │  affidavit │ /api/extract                         │
│                                     │ ─────────► │  pdf text layer → LLM vision → valid.│
│ Web Worker: location ingest         │            │ /api/geocode (NYC GeoSearch + cache) │
│  Timeline.json (Android/iOS), CSV,  │  windowed  │ /api/analyze                         │
│  manual → LocationFix[]             │  fixes     │  engine: feasibility, description,   │
│  window(±3h of each claim) ────────►│ ─────────► │  NY timing rules, verdict            │
│ Full history NEVER leaves device    │            │ /api/advocate/analyze                │
│                                     │            │ /api/documents/packet (WeasyPrint)   │
│ IndexedDB: optional local case save │  ◄──────── │ No DB. No persistence. Redacted logs.│
└─────────────────────────────────────┘            └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```
Decisions (do not relitigate):
- **Stateless server, no database.** Privacy and leanness. Case state lives in the browser and in the exported bundle.
- **Single deployable:** Docker multi-stage build; FastAPI serves the built SvelteKit static bundle. One Render web service.
- **LLM used in exactly one place:** affidavit field extraction. Engine, rules, verdicts and documents are deterministic Python. Documents are Jinja2 templates filled from user-confirmed fields.
- **Engine in Python, once.** Frontend never reimplements engine logic. Frontend sends windowed fixes only.
- **Types generated, not hand-copied:** backend OpenAPI → `openapi-typescript` → `frontend/src/lib/api/schema.d.ts`.

## 10. Data contracts (backend/app/domain/models.py) — implement exactly
```python
class ServiceMethod(StrEnum): PERSONAL = "308_1"; SUBSTITUTE = "308_2"; AFFIX_AND_MAIL = "308_4"; UNKNOWN = "unknown"
class LatLng(BaseModel): lat: float = Field(ge=-90, le=90); lng: float = Field(ge=-180, le=180)

class PersonDescription(BaseModel):
    sex: Literal["male","female","unknown"] | None = None
    age_min: int | None = None; age_max: int | None = None
    height_in_min: int | None = None; height_in_max: int | None = None
    weight_lb_min: int | None = None; weight_lb_max: int | None = None
    hair: str | None = None
    raw_text: str | None = None          # verbatim description block as extracted

class ServiceAttempt(BaseModel):
    at: AwareDatetime; address: str; location: LatLng | None = None
    outcome: Literal["served","affixed","not_home","refused","other"]

class Affidavit(BaseModel):
    index_number: str | None; court: str | None; plaintiff: str | None; defendant_name: str
    server_name: str | None; server_license: str | None; agency_license: str | None
    method: ServiceMethod
    served_at: AwareDatetime                 # the claimed completed service (delivery or affixing)
    served_address: str; served_location: LatLng | None = None
    recipient_name: str | None = None; recipient_relationship: str | None = None
    recipient_description: PersonDescription | None = None
    attempts: list[ServiceAttempt] = []      # prior attempts (308(4) due diligence)
    mailing_date: date | None = None; mailing_address: str | None = None
    proof_filed_date: date | None = None
    source_sha256: str
    field_confidence: dict[str, float] = {}  # 0..1 per field, from extractor
    user_confirmed: bool = False             # analysis refuses unconfirmed affidavits

class FixKind(StrEnum): VISIT="visit"; PATH="path"; TRANSACTION="transaction"; MANUAL="manual"
class LocationFix(BaseModel):
    t: AwareDatetime; t_end: AwareDatetime | None = None   # set for VISIT and MANUAL intervals
    loc: LatLng; accuracy_m: float | None = None
    kind: FixKind; source: str; label: str | None = None    # e.g. "Timeline visit: WORK"

class HouseholdMember(BaseModel):
    label: str; sex: Literal["male","female","other"] | None = None
    age: int | None = None; height_in: int | None = None; is_defendant: bool = False

class Severity(StrEnum): STRONG="strong"; MODERATE="moderate"; INFO="info"
class Finding(BaseModel):
    code: str                       # e.g. "F-PRISM", "F-VISIT", "F-DESC", "R-T1", "R-D1"
    severity: Severity; title: str; detail: str           # plain language, from copy templates
    numbers: dict[str, float | str] = {}; legal_ref: str | None = None   # L-id from bible §5

class ClaimTier(StrEnum): CONTRADICTED="contradicted"; CONSISTENT="consistent"; NO_DATA="no_data"; INCONCLUSIVE="inconclusive"
class ClaimVerdict(BaseModel):
    claim_ref: str                  # "served_at" or "attempt[i]"
    claimed_at: AwareDatetime; claimed_location: LatLng
    tier: ClaimTier; nearest_fix_km: float | None; required_speed_kmh: float | None
    fixes_used: list[LocationFix]

class Deadlines(BaseModel):
    knowledge_date: date | None; judgment_entry_date: date | None
    cplr_317_deadline: date | None; cplr_317_outer_limit: date | None; note: str

class AnalyzeRequest(BaseModel):
    affidavit: Affidavit; fixes: list[LocationFix]; household: list[HouseholdMember] = []
    knowledge_date: date | None = None; judgment_entry_date: date | None = None
class CaseAnalysis(BaseModel):
    affidavit: Affidavit; verdicts: list[ClaimVerdict]; findings: list[Finding]
    overall: ClaimTier; deadlines: Deadlines; params_version: str; engine_version: str; generated_at: AwareDatetime

# Advocate
class ServiceRecord(BaseModel):
    server_id: str; at: AwareDatetime; loc: LatLng; address: str | None = None
    case_ref: str | None = None; outcome: str | None = None; recipient_desc: str | None = None
```

## 11. Engine specification (deterministic; all thresholds in `engine/params.py`, versioned)
```python
PARAMS_VERSION = "2026.09-v1"
MATCH_RADIUS_KM = 0.30          # plus fix accuracy_m/1000 when present
VISIT_TOLERANCE_MIN = 10        # a visit covers T if t-10min <= T <= t_end+10min
SEARCH_WINDOW_H = 3             # fixes considered within ±3h of the claim
V_STRONG_KMH = 80               # door-to-door NYC; deliberately generous → conservative
V_MODERATE_KMH = 40
ADV_SIMULTANEOUS_MIN = 5; ADV_SIMULTANEOUS_KM = 2.0
ADV_MAX_PER_HOUR = 12
TZ = "America/New_York"
```
### 11.1 Claim feasibility (per claim: `served_at` and each attempt)
1. Normalize all times to aware UTC; display in `America/New_York`. Affidavit times without offset are localized with `zoneinfo`; on DST-ambiguous/non-existent local times, evaluate **both** interpretations and take the **weaker** tier (conservative).
2. **Visit test.** If a VISIT or MANUAL interval covers T (± tolerance): d = haversine(visit.loc, P). d > radius → `CONTRADICTED` (Finding `F-VISIT`, STRONG). d ≤ radius → `CONSISTENT`.
3. **Space-time prism test** (Hägerstrand time geography). Take the nearest fix before T (t1,p1) and after T (t2,p2) within the window. Required speeds: v1 = d(p1,P)/(T−t1), v2 = d(P,p2)/(t2−T). Guard: if |T−t| < 60s use v = ∞ when d > radius, else 0. vreq = max(v1, v2).
   - vreq > V_STRONG → `CONTRADICTED` (F-PRISM, STRONG)
   - V_MODERATE < vreq ≤ V_STRONG → `CONTRADICTED` (F-PRISM, MODERATE)
   - any fix within radius of P within ±15 min → `CONSISTENT`
   - else `INCONCLUSIVE`
   - only one side available → evaluate that side only.
4. No fixes in window → `NO_DATA`.
5. Precedence: visit test result wins over prism; CONSISTENT evidence at T overrides a MODERATE contradiction (report both as findings).
6. Overall = strongest CONTRADICTED across claims, else CONSISTENT if the main claim is consistent, else NO_DATA/INCONCLUSIVE.

Invariants (property tests):
- Moving any fix farther from P never lowers the contradiction severity.
- Shifting every timestamp by the same Δ changes nothing.
- Adding a fix far outside the window changes nothing.
- A fix exactly at P at exactly T always yields CONSISTENT.

### 11.2 Description check (`F-DESC`)
- 308(1) personal service: compare `recipient_description` to the household member with `is_defendant=True`.
- 308(2): compare to all household members. If no member matches, finding: "Nobody in your household matches the description of the person who supposedly accepted the papers."
- Match = every provided field compatible: sex equal; age within [age_min−5, age_max+5]; height within [min−2in, max+2in]. Missing fields are ignored, never counted as mismatch.
- Severity MODERATE (it depends on user-supplied roster). Never use or request photos.

### 11.3 NY timing and diligence rules (`engine/rules_ny.py`)
| Rule | Applies | Check | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-T1 | 308(2), 308(4) | mailing_date missing | MODERATE ("The affidavit doesn't show the required mailing.") |
| R-T2 | 308(2), 308(4) | abs(mailing_date − served_at.date) > 20 days | STRONG, L2/L3 |
| R-T3 | 308(2), 308(4) | proof_filed_date present and > 20 days after later of delivery/mailing | MODERATE, L2/L3 |
| R-D1 | 308(4) | attempts < 3, or attempts on < 2 distinct days, or all attempts inside Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00 local | MODERATE, "flag for review", L4 |
| R-D2 | 308(4) | any prior attempt itself `CONTRADICTED` by the user's data | STRONG |
| R-M1 | any | method UNKNOWN after user confirmation | INFO |

### 11.4 Advocate patterns (`advocate/patterns.py`)
- Group by `server_id`, sort by time. For consecutive records compute distance, minutes, speed.
- `ImpossiblePair` if speed > V_STRONG or (minutes ≤ 5 and distance ≥ 2 km).
- Throughput: max completed services in any rolling 60-minute window; flag > 12.
- Repeated descriptions: normalized `recipient_desc` strings reused at ≥ 3 different addresses.
- risk_rank: sort servers by (impossible pairs desc, repeated descriptions desc, throughput desc).
- Complexity O(n log n) per server. Must handle 50k rows in < 3 s.

## 12. Extraction specification
Pipeline: `pdf_text` (pdfplumber text layer) → if text ≥ 200 chars, send text to LLM for structuring; else rasterize pages (pypdfium2, 200 dpi, max 3 pages) and send images → Pydantic-validated JSON → `validators.py`.
- Provider interface: `class Extractor(Protocol): async def extract(self, doc: ExtractInput) -> ExtractionResult`.
- Prompt returns JSON matching an `AffidavitDraft` schema (all fields optional + per-field confidence + `evidence_quote` per field = verbatim span from the document).
- Validators (deterministic): date sanity (service not in future, mailing not > 60 days from service), license number pattern (digits, 7), times parse, method inferred from keywords when LLM leaves it UNKNOWN ("suitable age and discretion" → 308_2; "affixing" / "door" → 308_4; "personally" → 308_1), every field's `evidence_quote` must appear in the source text layer (fuzzy ratio ≥ 90) when a text layer exists, else confidence capped at 0.6.
- `LLM_PROVIDER=none` → returns empty draft; UI shows manual entry form. The app must be fully usable without any LLM.
- Temperature 0, one retry on schema failure, 30 s timeout, never log document text.

## 13. Location ingest (browser Web Worker)
Parsers must handle:
- **Google Timeline, Android export:** `{ "semanticSegments": [...] }`; visits at `visit.topCandidate.placeLocation.latLng` as `"45.5230992°, -73.6889564°"`; paths at `timelinePath[].point` + `time`; segment `startTime`/`endTime` ISO with offsets. Also read `rawSignals[].position` if present (`LatLng`, `timestamp`, `accuracyMeters`).
- **Google Timeline, iOS export:** top-level array; `visit.topCandidate.placeLocation` as `"geo:40.7,-73.9"`; `activity.start` / `activity.end` as `geo:` strings; `startTime`/`endTime`.
- **Card/bank CSV:** user maps columns (date, time, merchant, optional address). Rows with address → geocode via `/api/geocode` (address only, no amounts sent). Kind TRANSACTION, accuracy 500 m.
- **Manual entries:** "I was at work from 8:00 to 20:00 at <address>". Kind MANUAL.
- Robustness: stream-parse files up to 200 MB without freezing UI; reject unknown shapes with a clear message; dedupe identical fixes.
- `window.ts`: given claim times, keep only fixes within ±3 h of any claim. Only windowed fixes are sent to `/api/analyze`. UI shows "Sending N of M location points (only around the claimed times)".

## 14. UX specification
Mobile-first. Max reading level ~ grade 7. One primary action per screen.
1. **Landing:** headline "Frozen bank account? Never got court papers?" Sub: "Check whether the process server's sworn story matches where you really were." Buttons: *Start check* · *See a demo* · *I'm a legal advocate*.
2. **Step 1 — Your court papers:** upload affidavit of service (photo/PDF). "Where do I get this?" expandable (from the court file / Court Help Center). Extracted fields shown as an editable card; low-confidence fields highlighted amber with the source quote. User must tick "These details match my papers" to continue. Ask: "When did you first find out about the judgment?"
3. **Step 2 — Where were you?:** three tiles (Google Timeline · Card or bank statement · Type it in). Per-OS export instructions. Privacy badge: "Your location file is read on this device. We only use the few hours around the claimed time."
4. **Step 3 — Who lives with you? (optional):** simple roster rows.
5. **Result:** verdict card (tier headline + one-sentence reason with the key number, e.g. "At 7:42 PM your phone was 14.2 km away. Getting there would take 171 km/h."). Map: red pin = claimed service, blue trail = your data ±90 min, time scrubber. Findings list (strong first). "What this means / what it doesn't." Deadline clock (CPLR 317). Next steps: download Evidence Packet, download Draft Affidavit, where to get help, "Ask the plaintiff's lawyer for the server's GPS record".
6. **Advocate:** upload CSV/XLSX, map columns, table of servers ranked by risk, click → map of that server's day with impossible edges in red and a pair list; export CSV + PDF report.
7. **Methodology** (thresholds, eval numbers, limitations, sources) and **Privacy** pages.
Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigable, map has a text alternative table.

## 15. Documents
- **Evidence Packet PDF:** cover summary (verdict, key numbers), map image (captured client-side via `map.getCanvas().toDataURL()`, `preserveDrawingBuffer: true`), claim table, findings with legal refs, fixes table (only windowed), methodology box (params version, engine version), SHA-256 of affidavit file and of the fixes payload, generation timestamp, disclaimer.
- **Draft Supporting Affidavit** (to attach to the court's official Order to Show Cause form, not replace it): Jinja2 template; numbered paragraphs built only from confirmed fields and findings; grounds: lack of personal jurisdiction (L5) and request for traverse hearing; alternative CPLR 317 paragraph only when method ≠ 308(1) and deadline not passed; request for stay of enforcement; exhibit list (A: affidavit of service, B: evidence packet). Signature/notary block left blank. Header watermark "DRAFT".
- No LLM in documents. Ever.

## 16. Privacy, safety, security
- Stateless server; uploads processed in memory; temp files deleted in `finally`.
- Logs: request id, route, latency, status. Never names, addresses, coordinates, document text.
- Size limits: 15 MB upload, 5,000 fixes per analyze request, 50,000 advocate rows.
- CORS locked to the deployed origin. Basic rate limit (in-memory token bucket per IP).
- Fixtures and demo data: synthetic names, synthetic cases, real public NYC street addresses only as geography. Every demo screen shows a "Synthetic demo data" chip.
